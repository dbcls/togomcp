## Reading

- The judge change (Opus 4.6 to 4.7) raised both arms by a similar amount (With Guide: TogoMCP +0.72, no-tool +0.74), so it leaves the TogoMCP − no-tool Δ almost unchanged (-0.02). It inflates absolute scores, not the effect size.
- The no-tool baseline's rise from rev0 to the paper batch (With Guide +1.11) is mostly the judge (+0.74); the rest (+0.37) is run-to-run answer variation, since that arm never uses TogoMCP.
- The system improvement between the batches is the TogoMCP arm's gain beyond that control: With Guide +0.76 points (TogoMCP +1.12 vs no-tool +0.37 under the same judge). A naive rev0 to paper comparison of the TogoMCP arm (+1.84) would overstate it by about 1.1 points.
- The judge change leaves the Δ nearly unchanged for with_guide, ng1 and ng2 (-0.02, +0.00, +0.11) but not for no_mie (+0.48): Opus 4.7 scores no-MIE TogoMCP answers relatively higher than 4.6 did. Comparisons involving no_mie across judges need that correction; within one judge they are unaffected.
- no_mie also has the largest net system effect between the batches.
- Questions and ideal answers are textually identical in both batches for every condition, so question edits do not confound these differences.
